03 / Before you drop in

A few things to know.

Built by a player, for the next build.

What does the StarRupture calculator do?+

The StarRupture (Star Rupture) calculator turns target items and output per minute into a production chain. It shows the raw materials, crafting recipes, building counts, and power needed for your plan.

Which tool should I use: My Bases or Planner?+

Use Planner to work independently with production plans and compare recipes without creating a base. Use My Bases to organize plans and buildings by location, group power, and track resource transfers across your bases.

Do I need an account?+

No account, no subscription. Open a tool and start planning. Rupture Planner is free and open source.

Where are my plans saved?+

Your bases and plans are saved locally in this browser. Export a backup in Global settings to keep a copy or move your work to another device.

Can I choose my game version?+

Yes. Use the version selector in the tool’s header to match your StarRupture game data. Changing the interface language keeps game item and building names unchanged.
